Frequently asked questions

What are credits?

Each AI edit costs credits depending on the quality you choose: Fast costs 1 credit, Balanced 2, and Best 8. Your plan refills credits every month, and subscribers can buy top-ups anytime.

Do I need my own API key?

No. The AI models are included in every plan — even Free. Sign in, pick a plan, and start editing.

Are my photos used to train AI?

No. Photos are sent to our AI provider only to perform the edit you asked for, and are not used for model training. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Which Macs are supported?

Revela runs on modern Macs running the latest macOS. It's built natively in Swift for a fast, calm experience.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Subscriptions are managed through the App Store and can be cancelled anytime in your Apple ID settings. You keep your plan until the end of the billing period.
