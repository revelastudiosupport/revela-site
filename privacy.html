Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Revela (“we”, “us”) handles your information when you use the Revela app for macOS, iOS and iPadOS (the “App”) and this website. We designed Revela to collect as little data as possible: your photo library stays on your device, and we only process what is needed to deliver the AI edits you request.

1. Who we are

Revela is operated by Casper Steenhoven, based in the Netherlands. For any privacy question or request, contact us at revelastudiosupport@gmail.com.

2. What we collect and why

DataPurposeWhere it goes
Account identifier (via Sign in with Apple) Creating your account and tracking your credit balance Our backend (Supabase)
Email address (only if you choose to share it via Sign in with Apple; you may use Apple's “Hide My Email”) Account identification and support Our backend (Supabase)
Photos you submit for an AI edit, plus your edit instruction Performing the edit you requested Transmitted to our AI provider (OpenAI) via our server, processed, and returned. Not stored on our servers.
Purchase and subscription status Unlocking your plan and managing credits Apple (App Store) and RevenueCat
Credit usage (number and quality of edits) Enforcing your plan's monthly allowance and preventing abuse Our backend (Supabase)

What we do not collect: we do not run analytics or advertising trackers, we do not access your photo library beyond the images you explicitly submit for editing, and we never sell your data.

3. Your photos

  • Your photo library is stored locally on your device. Browsing, organizing, and viewing photos happens entirely on your device. On iPhone and iPad, photos you import are copied into Revela's private storage on that device (excluded from iCloud backup); removing a photo from Revela deletes that copy, never the original in Photos or Files.
  • When you request an AI edit, the selected photo and your instruction are sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards them to OpenAI to generate the edit.
  • We do not keep copies of your photos on our servers. OpenAI processes API content under its API data usage policies and does not use API content to train its models.

4. Third-party services

We rely on a small number of processors to run Revela:

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple and App Store payments (privacy policy)
  • Supabase — authentication and credit ledger hosting (privacy policy)
  • OpenAI — AI image editing (privacy policy)
  • RevenueCat — subscription management (privacy policy)

Each of these providers processes data only as needed to provide their service to us.

5. Payments

All payments are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. We receive only your subscription status (which plan is active) via RevenueCat so the App can unlock the right features.

6. Data retention

  • Photos and edit instructions: processed transiently for the edit; not retained on our servers.
  • Account and credit data: retained while your account exists.
  • Deletion: in Revela, open Settings → Account → Delete account…. Confirmation immediately removes your Revela account and cloud credit balance from our backend. It does not delete photos or files stored locally on your devices.
  • Purchases: Apple and RevenueCat may retain transaction and subscription records where required for billing, fraud prevention, tax, or other legal obligations. Deleting your Revela account does not cancel an active App Store subscription.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. To delete your Revela account, use Settings → Account → Delete account… in the App. For other privacy requests, email us at revelastudiosupport@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority — in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Our legal bases for processing are: performance of a contract (delivering the App and the edits you request), and legitimate interest (preventing abuse of the credit system).

8. Security

All communication between the App, our servers, and our providers is encrypted in transit (TLS). Access to our backend is restricted, and per-user data is protected by row-level security so one user can never access another's data.

9. Children

Revela is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the latest revision, and material changes will be highlighted in the App or on this website.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email revelastudiosupport@gmail.com.
